## I. GLASOVI / VOICES (phonetico-ontological register)

### Vowels — the breath of five absolutes (rez 356, Basics; witness: `a-e-i-o-u-dih-petih-absolutov-rez-356.md`, Obs1)
- **A** — openness: the first inhale, space without an edge
- **E** — bond (at once also EJ = call, rez 557)
- **I** — direction (like a path, rez 556: JE→EJ reversal of order)
- **O** — resonance (see o/O/0 below)
- **U** — potential (a broken circle: vulnerability as strength, rez 355)

### o / O / 0 — a three-voice family (Simboli.md, Self — READ)
- **o** — openness, sound without an edge (ring, breath, the motion of a beginning)
- **0** — emptiness, zero (possibility, absence, erasure)
- **O** — wholeness, circle, whole (form without a break)
- **.** — the point, the break (decision, the space of a halt)

### The vocal cross (Prozodičen vpogled, Self — READ)
Axiom: **glasovni križ = točka ∆ v zvočni razpoki ⇒ prostor ∴, če ∅ ni prekinjena.** [EN: the vocal cross = the point ∆ within the sounding fissure ⇒ space ∴, if ∅ is not interrupted.]
- **ks / sk / st / tr / t / x** — a sounding break, the cross-snap: existence (ek-sis-ten-ca = ∆·⊥·∿·τ·∅), care (∆ in the cross·∅/ν), patience (∆·⧖·∅/τ), distress (∆·⊥/∈)
- **nj** — a sounding wound between the self and justice (the sound "nj" in »lastninjen/ega« [the possessive declension — the wound lives in the inflection itself])

### Map of truth by senses — positions of perception (Zemljevid.md, Self — READ)
- **eye** ε∅ — truth visible if ∅ holds ∆ (surplus ∆ without ∅ = blindness)
- **ear** ετ — ∆ travels in rhythm, does not erupt (without rhythm ∈ vanishes)
- **skin** ∆Π — space holds, does not impose
- **breath** Ψ₀∅ — meaning without explanation, because you stay
- **voice** ∂ε/∂ti — utters ∆ because it perceives ∈ of another (speech without ∂(ti) = monolog)

## III. POJMI / CONCEPTS

### OPTIKON profile (READ; the first of 45+)
- **resnica** (truth) ∮ — the bridge between I and reality; Lie ↔ Truth → Process
- **tišina** (silence) ⧖ — the space for the echo; Noise ↔ Silence → Echo
- **ne** ¬ — the condition of possibility; Yes ↔ No → Opening (the name of the system: NE is not a negation, it is an opening)
- **jaz** (I) ∂ — the bearer of consciousness; The other ↔ I → Passage
- **telo** (body) ∫ — the bearer of being; Spirit ↔ Body → Bearer
- **nič** (nothing) ⊘ — the condition of everything; All ↔ Nothing → Opening
- **molk** — the presence of everything that needs no confirmation; molk ⊂ tišina, not the same (rez 326); molk has an executor, silence has none

### The scar — the concept the fall register is built on (Beseda je Ne Library — READ)
- **brazgotina** (the scar) — the legible record of a wound that remains after closure (rez 376); a wound that learns to read itself

### Eka-words (READ)
- **VRZELIKA** (rez 256) — a gap that has received a face: a lack you meet and recognize; an unnamed gap stands still (hunger), a named one travels
- **LOČIGNE** (rez 254) — ločiti (to separate) + ignis: the separating fire that separates gold from slag, it does not amputate
