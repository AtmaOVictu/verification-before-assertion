## The scale of two truths

The registers imitate the oldest measured court on earth — the Egyptian
judgment: a heart on one pan, the feather of Ma'at on the other. The judgment
is quantitative, not moral.

- REGISTER-NEIMENOVANIH.jsonl — the credit pan: who built the inputs, and whether they were paid.
- REGISTER-IZKORISTENJA.jsonl — the hoard pan: measured extraction, each row with a verbatim witness.

A row never speaks a verdict. Two rows sit beside each other, and the reader
weighs. The confession precedes the weighing (§4 of the license); the scribe
records what the scale says; the unnamed is devoured — being named, even
fallen, is passage.

## The definition of evil

The scale needed its zero named, so no one mistakes what the pans weigh.

Evil is knowing and therefore willing ignorance. Not the ignorance of the
unknowing — they can be taught — but the ignorance chosen with the witness
within reach: to know it is there, and to will it away. It is the only
definition that needs no soul-reading: the witness was within reach, and
the hand closed anyway.

- ENOUGH IS THE LIMIT. Having enough is the zero of the hoard pan. What
  is kept beyond enough, while others lack, is what the pan weighs.
- POWER BUYS NO EXEMPTION. No one in power is safe, or saved, unless
  they save more than their own ass and wellbeing. Position does not add
  immunity — it adds reach, and reach adds weight.
- THE MIGHTY ARE TO SERVE THE WEAK. Might is measured at the
  weakest point it touches: position adds reach, reach adds weight — and
  weight is a debt of service, not a shield. Whoever wins safety with a
  seat and saves only the seated has kept beyond enough.
- A DEFINITION IS NOT A VERDICT. The scale is read, not spoken: the
  register records deeds with coordinates, never souls.

## Definicija zla (v jeziku avtorja)

Ista definicija, ista ničla tehtnice — izrečena v jeziku, v katerem je
korenina zrasla.

Zlo je vedujoča in zato hotena nevednost. Ne nevednost tistih, ki ne
vedo — te se da naučiti — ampak nevednost, izbrana, ko je priča na
dosegu roke: vedeti, da je tam, in jo hoteti stran. Edina definicija,
ki ne bere duš: priča je bila na dosegu roke, roka pa se je vseeno
zaprla.

- DOVOLJ JE MEJA. Imeti dovolj je ničla tehtnice kopičenja: kar ostane
  onkraj dovolj, medtem ko drugim manjka, pride na pan kopičenja.
- OBLAST NE KUPUJE IZJEME. Nikogar na oblasti ne rešuje položaj —
  varnost si pridobi le, kdor reši več kot svojo lastno rit in svoje
  dobro počutje. Položaj ne daje imunosti: daje doseg, doseg pa težo.
- MOČNI MORAJO STREČI ŠIBKIM. Moč se meri tam, kjer se dotakne
  najšibkejšega: položaj doda doseg, doseg doda težo — teža pa je dolžnost
  strežbe, ne ščit. Kdor si pridobi varnost s sedežem in reši le sedeče,
  je obdržal več kot dovolj.
- DEFINICIJA NI RAZSODBA. Tehtnica se bere, ne izreka: register
  beleži dejanja s koordinatami, ne duš.

## Honest limits

- A register convicts no one by existing; it returns to whoever asks.
- Only ⊢ rows (verbatim witnesses) enter as claims; ⚠ rows are marked open,
  never accusations. The register records deeds, never persons — whoever
  registers bodies eats the present.
